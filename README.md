# Local Pro Connect

Act as an expert UX/UI Designer and Frontend React Developer. Please build a modern, high-converting, and deeply SEO-optimized landing page for a hyper-local Home Services App (like Urban Company, but with a unique bidding model). The design should be clean, trustworthy, and mobile-first, using Tailwind CSS and Lucide React icons.
BUSINESS MODEL (Must be clearly communicated in the copy):
For Customers: 100% Free. Post a job, get bids from nearby verified pros, and hire the best.
For Service Providers: No subscriptions, no hidden commissions. Pay a small flat 'Access Fee' ONLY when you win a booking.
Current Promo: "Get your first 3 bookings absolutely FREE! Plus, refer another provider and get 3 more FREE bookings."
PAGE STRUCTURE & SECTIONS REQUIRED:
1. Sticky Navbar:
Logo on the left.
Links: Home, How it Works, For Providers, FAQ, Contact.
Call to Action (CTA) button: "Download App".
2. Hero Section (Split Audience Focus):
Headline (H1 for SEO): "Your Local Experts, Just a Tap Away."
Subheadline: "Get plumbing, electrical, and home repairs done instantly. 100% Free for customers. Providers pay only for confirmed jobs."
Dual CTAs:
Primary Button: "Download Android App" (with Google Play icon).
Secondary Button (Disabled/Muted): "iOS App - Coming Soon" (with Apple icon).
Image/Hero Graphic: A friendly, verified local handyman smiling, alongside a happy family or a mockup of the mobile app.
3. Provider Promotional Banner (High Visibility):
A distinct, eye-catching banner highlighting the provider offer: "Are you a service professional? Join today and get your First 3 Jobs FREE! Refer a friend to earn 3 more. Zero subscription fees."
Button: "Join as a Pro".
4. How It Works (Step-by-Step):
Toggle or Side-by-Side view for 'Customers' vs 'Providers'.
Customers: 1. Post a Request -> 2. Receive Offers -> 3. Accept & Relax.
Providers: 1. See Nearby Jobs -> 2. Send Your Bid -> 3. Pay Only if Accepted -> 4. Complete the Job.
5. Why Choose Us? (Features for SEO):
Icons with short text: Verified Professionals, 50km Hyper-Local Radius, Transparent Pricing, Zero Customer Fees.
6. App Download Section (Bottom CTA):
Big bold section showing app screens.
"Available now for Android. iOS dropping soon."
Google Play Store Badge (Active link) and App Store Badge (Coming Soon state).
7. SEO-Optimized Footer (Crucial for extra pages):
Quick Links: About Us, Contact Us, FAQ.
Legal Links: Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Cancellation & Refund Policy.
Social Media icons.
Copyright text: "© 2026 [App Name]. All rights reserved."
TECHNICAL & SEO REQUIREMENTS:
Use proper semantic HTML (<main>, <section>, <article>, <header>, <footer>).
Use strict heading hierarchy (One H1, followed by logical H2s and H3s).
Ensure high contrast ratios and accessibility (aria-labels for icons/buttons).
Keep the design fast, lightweight, and extremely responsive for mobile devices.
Use a trustworthy color palette (e.g., Deep Blue and vibrant Orange/Green for CTAs).
Please generate the full landing page structure with beautiful dummy text that fits the context perfectly. Also, create placeholders or separate simple components for the Privacy Policy and FAQ pages.
